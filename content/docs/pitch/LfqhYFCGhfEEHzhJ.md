{{<hw t = "banner" header = "Problem" />}}

You've done the work, written the import/export tools, made your own normalized metadata schemas and polished the UI
until the customers can see their reflection in the shiny loveliness of the downloadable app.

What if you could sell that metadata conversion as a subscription to a wider audience? What if highlighting those
capabilities became a small revenue generator that led to personal recommendation rather than marketing costs?

{{<hw t = "banner" header = "Appetite" />}}

Would you be willing to extract and refactor 2 of your supported camera metadata formats into serverless Spin
applications. These would be the first PAYGO apps to be tested in the Proof of Concept for Q1 next year.

{{<hw t = "banner" header = "Solution" />}}

The {{metarex}} workflow would be used in a non-hedge workflow MAM to identify that normalisation can take place,
authentication & a micropayment would occur and the 3rd party MAM would receive validated, verified data in a normalised
format.

Purpose and Process

A pitch is used to present a "bet" to decision-makers, who choose which pitches become projects for the upcoming build cycle. The pitch trades detail for clarity-it's specific enough to show the core idea and risks, but avoids granular specs or design mandates so that product teams retain autonomy during the build.
